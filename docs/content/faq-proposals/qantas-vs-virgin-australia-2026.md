# FAQ proposal — Qantas vs Virgin Australia in 2026: Which Airline Wins?

Slug: `qantas-vs-virgin-australia-2026` · Status: **PROPOSED — not in Strapi** · Grounding: article body only

## Q1: Which airline is better in 2026, Qantas or Virgin Australia?
The overall call for 2026: choose Qantas for long-haul flying or the deepest network, and Virgin Australia for mostly domestic east coast travel where price and a friendlier cabin matter. Qantas wins on regional reach and international routes, while Virgin's domestic fares often undercut Qantas by AUD 30–80 on trunk routes.

## Q2: Is Virgin Australia cheaper than Qantas?
On Sydney–Melbourne economy, Virgin tends to sit AUD 20–60 below Qantas in same-day comparisons — sometimes the difference between an AUD 180 and AUD 230 one-way fare. Sydney–Perth is roughly line-ball, and Virgin frequently undercuts both Jetstar and Qantas on Brisbane–Bali weekday departures. Most domestic sales drop on Thursday mornings.

## Q3: Which frequent flyer program is better, Qantas Frequent Flyer or Velocity?
Neither is strictly better — they excel at different things. Qantas Frequent Flyer earns across oneworld, and Gold status brings worldwide oneworld Sapphire lounge access, with Classic Reward business seats from roughly 41,900 points Sydney–Singapore. Velocity earns on partners including Qatar, United, ANA, and Singapore Airlines, and the late-2025 Qatar partnership opened new Europe redemptions.

## Q4: Who has better airport lounges, Qantas or Virgin Australia?
Qantas has more lounges in more places, but Virgin's are nicer per square foot — the Brisbane Virgin Lounge is described as the best domestic lounge in the country. Paid membership costs around AUD 600 annually for Qantas Club versus AUD 550 for Virgin. For mostly east coast domestic flying, Virgin's membership is the better per-visit deal.

## Q5: Where does Virgin Australia fly internationally?
Virgin's international network is short-haul: Bali, Fiji, Queenstown, Samoa, Vanuatu, and Tokyo Haneda, which launched in June 2023. Anything beyond means connecting onto partners — Qatar Airways via Doha for Europe, United via the US west coast, plus Singapore Airlines, ANA, Etihad, and Air Canada. Qantas remains the default for other long-haul destinations.
