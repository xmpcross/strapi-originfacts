## Q1: When are flights to Bangkok cheapest in 2026?
The cheapest 2026 windows are late April through mid-May right after Songkran, late August through late September during monsoon season, and the first two weeks of November. LAX–BKK round-trips drop into the $580–$680 range during these periods. Avoid mid-December through January 5, Chinese New Year week, and Songkran itself.

## Q2: How far in advance should you book a flight to Bangkok?
For economy, the cheapest fares typically appear 70–100 days before departure, and booking inside four weeks is painful without a flash sale. Premium economy is best 90–120 days out, and business class four to six months ahead. Tuesday or Wednesday departures and returns shave $40–$120 off most itineraries.

## Q3: Should you book Bangkok flights through an online travel agency or directly with the airline?
Booking directly on the airline's website is recommended, even after using Google Flights' date grid to find cheap dates. When schedule changes or missed connections happen, online travel agencies can leave travelers on hold while blaming the airline, whereas airlines fix issues directly. The price difference is usually only $5–$25.

## Q4: Can booking separate tickets through Taipei save money on Bangkok flights?
Yes. Searching segments separately can beat through fares: a round-trip LAX–TPE on EVA might run $520 while TPE–BKK costs $180–$240 round-trip, totaling under $760 with a free Taipei stopover option. The risk is that separate tickets do not protect connections, so a minimum four-hour buffer in Taipei is advised.

## Q5: Are budget long-haul carriers like ZIPAIR worth it for Bangkok trips?
Budget carriers like ZIPAIR and Scoot make sense for travelers going carry-on only with flexible schedules, and only when the total comes in at least $200 below a full-service carrier. They are a poor fit when seat selection or meals matter, layovers are under four hours, or savings are just $50–$100.
