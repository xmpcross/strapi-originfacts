# FAQ proposal — Qantas Domestic Flights: An Honest Review and Comparison

## Q1: How much do Qantas domestic flights cost?
On the Sydney–Melbourne route, Qantas Economy can dip under $150 during sales booked weeks ahead, but a mid-week fare booked two to three weeks out typically runs $180–$280 depending on time of day. Last-minute Monday morning peak departures can pass $400. Domestic Business starts around $500–$600 one-way and can exceed $1,000 on Sydney–Perth.

## Q2: Is Qantas worth the premium over Jetstar on domestic routes?
It depends on what is being bought. Qantas includes complimentary snacks and drinks, satellite Wi-Fi across its 737 fleet, historically better on-time performance, and lounge infrastructure, while Jetstar charges for everything beyond a small cup of water. For a leisure trip booked eight weeks out with zero chance of changes, Jetstar's base fare is harder to argue against.

## Q3: How does Qantas compare with Virgin Australia domestically?
Virgin Australia often prices $10–$40 cheaper than Qantas on comparable flexible fares with a similar cabin product, and both offer 30-inch seat pitch on their main aircraft. Qantas's advantages over Virgin are narrower than over Jetstar — mainly lie-flat Business on the long Sydney–Perth sector, points redemptions, and airports where Virgin's lounge footprint is thinner.

## Q4: What baggage is included on Qantas domestic fares?
Sale fares include carry-on only — one piece up to 7kg. Flex fares add a 23kg checked bag, and Business includes 32kg. Adding a bag to a Sale fare typically costs $30–$50 depending on route, cheaper at booking than at the airport, so the gap between Sale and Flex often narrows once baggage is added.

## Q5: How do you earn Qantas Frequent Flyer status on domestic flights?
A Sydney–Melbourne economy flight earns 5 status credits on a flex fare, against thresholds of 300 for Bronze, 700 for Silver, and 1,400 for Gold. Domestic-only travellers need high flight volume or higher fare classes to reach meaningful status; the program is more useful combined with international Qantas flying or partner credit cards. Book directly for cleaner accrual.
