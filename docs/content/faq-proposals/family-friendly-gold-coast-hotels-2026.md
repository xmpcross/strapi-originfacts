# FAQ proposal — 8 Family-Friendly Gold Coast Hotels to Love in 2026

## Q1: What are the best family-friendly hotels on the Gold Coast?
Eight standouts include the JW Marriott Gold Coast Resort with its live-fish lagoon pool, Sea World Resort with unlimited park entry, RACV Royal Pines for value, The Star Grand for car-free stays, Peppers Soul and Hilton Residences for apartment space, Mantra on View on a budget, and InterContinental Sanctuary Cove for calm.

## Q2: How much do family rooms on the Gold Coast cost?
Typical shoulder-season rates in AUD range from $180–$260 per night at Mantra on View up to $480–$650 for a two-bedroom oceanfront apartment at Peppers Soul. Mid-range options include The Star Grand at $290–$420 and RACV Royal Pines at $260–$370. Expect 30–60% jumps during Queensland school holidays.

## Q3: When is the best time to book a Gold Coast family holiday?
Best value windows are early May, late October through late November, and the first two weeks of February, when weather stays warm and rates drop 30–50% below peak. Queensland school holidays push rates up 40–80%. Book big resorts four to six months ahead for holiday periods; otherwise six to eight weeks out is usually fine.

## Q4: Which Gold Coast hotel is best for theme parks?
Sea World Resort in Main Beach bundles unlimited Sea World entry into most packages, plus discounted entry to Movie World, Wet'n'Wild, and Paradise Country. At AUD $310–$450 per night including park passes, the maths usually beats buying tickets separately for stays of two nights or more, and an on-site monorail runs straight into the park.

## Q5: Can families stay on the Gold Coast without hiring a car?
Yes — The Star Grand in Broadbeach is the best base for families who hate driving, sitting a two-minute walk from Pacific Fair, four minutes from Broadbeach's patrolled beach, with the G:link tram stopping outside. By contrast, RACV Royal Pines and InterContinental Sanctuary Cove require rideshare or hire-car budgets.
