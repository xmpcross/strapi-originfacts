## Q1: Which luxury hotels in Europe are best for pets?
Three luxury standouts are highlighted: The Egerton House Hotel in London's Knightsbridge, which gives dogs a personalised welcome pack near Hyde Park's walking routes; Hotel Sacher in Vienna, offering custom bedding, food bowls, pet-sitting, and concierge-arranged veterinary visits; and Château de la Resle in Burgundy, where dogs roam extensive gardens near pet-friendly vineyards.

## Q2: Are there budget-friendly pet-friendly hotels in Europe?
Yes. Motel One, with properties across Germany, Austria, and beyond, offers consistent quality without pet fees and central locations that make city exploration straightforward. Citizenm hotels in various cities are modern, affordable, and genuinely welcoming to pets, with compact rooms suited to travellers who prioritise location over space.

## Q3: What should travelers check before booking a pet-friendly hotel?
Confirm pet policies directly with the hotel, since weight limits, breed restrictions, and additional fees vary significantly. Request a ground-floor room for easier bathroom breaks and emergencies, and ask about nearby parks, veterinary clinics, and pet supply shops. Booking well in advance matters too, because pet-friendly rooms fill quickly in peak summer months.

## Q4: How should pets be prepared for a European hotel stay?
Preparation is the key: ensure vaccinations and microchip details meet EU requirements, and consider travel insurance that covers pet-related incidents. During stays, bring familiar items like blankets and favourite toys, keep regular feeding and walking schedules, use do-not-disturb signs when briefly leaving pets alone, and keep pets leashed in common areas.
