# FAQ proposal — Airport vs City Car Rentals: Which One Actually Saves You Money?

Slug: `airport-vs-city-car-rentals-which-saves-money` · Status: **PROPOSED — not in Strapi** · Grounding: article body only

## Q1: Why are airport car rentals more expensive?
Every major U.S. airport charges rental companies a concession fee, typically 10-12% of the base rate, which is passed to customers as an airport concession recovery fee. A Customer Facility Charge — around $6-9 per day at LAX — plus state and local taxes can turn a $35/day compact into $55-65/day.

## Q2: When does a city rental location save the most money?
The price gap grows with rental length. A 24-hour rental might carry only $12-18 in airport surcharges, while a 7-day rental can add $80-130. One example: the same Enterprise compact cost $340 per week at O'Hare but under $250 at downtown Chicago's Wacker Drive location — same car class and dates.

## Q3: When should you rent at the airport instead?
Airport pickup wins when landing late or leaving early, since airport locations are typically open around the clock while city branches often close by 6 p.m. It also makes sense when a road trip starts immediately on arrival, when a business account or loyalty program neutralizes the fee difference, or for rentals of two days or fewer.

## Q4: How should you compare airport and city rental prices?
Start on Kayak or Autoslash, which aggregate airport and off-airport locations together, and set the search to "all nearby locations." Always compare full prices with taxes and fees, check the company's own site for city branches, factor in fuel policy, and read return hours — after-hours drop fees of $25-50 can erase savings.

## Q5: Do rideshare costs cancel out city rental savings?
Sometimes. The rule is to add the city rental price, the Uber or Lyft fare to pickup, and any time cost before deciding. Paying $30-40 in rideshare to save $60 over five days is still worthwhile; saving only $20 over two days probably is not. A useful threshold is a total price difference above $50.
