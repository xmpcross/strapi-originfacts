# Originfacts Editorial Methodology & Data Standards

At **Originfacts**, accuracy, transparency, and trust are at the core of everything we publish. As a travel research and information site, we help readers understand both the origin stories behind world destinations and the practical travel realities needed to plan trips today.

This document outlines our editorial standards, data verification process, research methodology, AI usage guidelines, and transparency policies.

---

## 1. What Are Our Core Editorial Principles?

### Why Focus on Origin-First Travel Insights?
Every destination has a history, culture, and geographic context. Before recommending where to stay or how to fly, our articles examine the origin of a place—its history, culture, and local reality—so travel decisions are built on authentic understanding.

### How Do We Ensure Fact-Grounded Travel Information?
Travel details change rapidly. We focus on verifiable facts: flight routes, airport amenities, visa requirements, hotel locations, and fare patterns. We do not publish unverified travel rumors or misleading clickbait.

### How Do We Maintain Commercial Transparency?
Originfacts is an independent publication supported by affiliate partnerships. When readers click affiliate links for flight searches or hotel bookings, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to the reader. Our editorial recommendations remain independent of affiliate relationships.

## 2. How Do We Research and Verify Travel Data?

Our content team follows a multi-stage verification methodology before any article or guide goes live:

1. **Topic Identification & Primary Briefing**  
   Editors identify travel topics based on reader interest, route changes, seasonal travel, and destination queries.

2. **Data Sourcing from Primary Entities & Verified Standards Bodies**  
   - **Airlines & Airports:** Sourced from official carrier regulatory filings, [IATA Passenger Standards](https://www.iata.org/en/programs/passenger/), [ICAO Civil Aviation Safety Data](https://www.icao.int/safety/Pages/default.aspx), and direct airport operations portals.  
   - **Routes, Fares & Schedules:** Cross-referenced with official aviation data feeds, the [U.S. Bureau of Transportation Statistics](https://www.bts.gov/topics/airlines-jet-fuel-and-transportation-data), and civil aviation statistics.  
   - **Passenger Rights & Safety Rules:** Verified against [U.S. Department of Transportation Air Consumer Protections](https://www.transportation.gov/airconsumer), [FAA PackSafe Hazardous Materials Rules](https://www.faa.gov/hazmat/packsafe), [EU Air Passenger Rights (EU261)](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm), and [Australian CASA Baggage Guidelines](https://www.casa.gov.au/passengers-and-can-i-pack-that).
   - **Destinations, Visas & Health:** Sourced from official national tourism authorities, [UN Tourism (UNWTO) Global Metrics](https://www.unwto.org/tourism-data/unwto-tourism-dashboard), [U.S. State Department Travel Advisories](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories.html), [CDC Travelers' Health Guidance](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel), [WHO International Travel Guidelines](https://www.who.int/ith), and [Australian Smartraveller](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/).
   - **Academic Research & Aviation Analytics:** Grounded in peer-reviewed aerospace and logistics research from the [MIT International Center for Air Transportation](https://icat.mit.edu/) and [Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University Research Division](https://erau.edu/research).

3. **Multi-Step Fact Verification**  
   Key data points—such as airport transport options, baggage rules, visa prerequisites, and peak travel windows—are verified against official primary sources.

4. **Continuous Content Audits**  
   Travel realities evolve. We routinely re-audit published guides to update deprecated routes, updated carrier policies, or changed airport facilities.

## 3. How Do We Use Responsible AI and Human Governance?

Originfacts uses advanced artificial intelligence tools to assist in data aggregation, research compilation, and initial drafting. However, **all content published on Originfacts is reviewed, fact-checked, and approved by human editors.**

- **AI as a Research Assistant:** AI models assist in organizing large datasets (such as airport amenities or historical timelines).
- **Mandatory Human Editorial Review:** No automated output is published without human review. Editors verify pricing estimates, check policy details, refine language, and confirm factual precision.
- **Image Disclosure:** Illustrative visual graphics produced using generative models are styled to represent general concepts and are never used to misrepresent real individuals or specific pricing offers.
