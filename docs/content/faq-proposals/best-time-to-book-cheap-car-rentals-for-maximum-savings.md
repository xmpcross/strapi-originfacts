# FAQ proposal — Best Time to Book Cheap Car Rentals for Maximum Savings

## Q1: When is the best time to book a car rental?
Rates follow a U-shaped curve, and 4–8 weeks out is often the sweet spot for standard trips: inventory is available, demand hasn't spiked, and weekly-rate discounts are accessible. Booking 3–6 months early yields decent but not lowest rates, while 1–2 weeks out prices climb, especially for weekends and airport locations.

## Q2: How does the re-book strategy lower car rental costs?
Book a fully refundable rate as soon as travel dates are known, then re-check prices weekly; whenever a lower rate appears, book it and cancel the original, repeating until about 48 hours before pickup. The approach has saved $80–$140 on a single week-long Las Vegas rental through just two re-bookings.

## Q3: Are airport car rentals more expensive than off-airport locations?
Yes: airport concession fees and facility charges can add 25–30% to the base rate, turning a $45-per-day economy car into $65. Off-airport locations cut costs substantially, though reaching them typically means a $20–$40 taxi or rideshare and shorter operating hours. The math usually favors off-airport for rentals of four or more days.

## Q4: Which memberships and discounts actually cut rental car costs?
Costco Travel consistently offers some of the lowest rates and includes additional-driver fees that otherwise run $10–$15 per day. AAA membership at $60–$70 per year unlocks Hertz, Budget, and Avis discounts that can offset the cost in one trip, while cards like Chase Sapphire Reserve include primary rental insurance, letting renters decline $15–$30-per-day counter coverage.

## Q5: What is the cheapest day of the week to pick up a rental car?
Tuesday and Wednesday pickups are historically the cheapest, particularly in business-heavy markets like Dallas/Fort Worth or Chicago O'Hare, while Sunday pickups carry a premium because families start vacation weeks then. Friday-evening-to-Monday-morning weekend specials can undercut a standard two-day rate, so shifting a flexible pickup by one day is worth testing.
