# FAQ proposal — Best Airlines to Fly to Bangkok in 2026: Honest Picks

## Q1: What is the best airline to fly to Bangkok from the US?
There is no US-Bangkok nonstop in 2026, so the connection city decides. From the West Coast, EVA Air via Taipei or ANA via Tokyo Narita are the top picks; from the East Coast, Qatar Airways via Doha or Japan Airlines via Tokyo Haneda. Economy round-trips typically run $1,100-1,900 in shoulder season.

## Q2: When is the cheapest time to fly to Bangkok?
The cheapest months are late April through early June (post-Songkran) and September into early October, when US round-trips can drop to $900-1,200 in economy. Mid-December through early January and the week around Songkran (April 12-16) add 40-60% to fares. Book 3-5 months out for economy, 6-9 months for business.

## Q3: Which airline has the best business class to Bangkok?
Qatar Airways' Qsuite, with direct aisle access, a sliding door, and the strongest food. In sales, JFK-Doha-Bangkok round-trips in Qsuite drop to roughly $4,500-5,500 — the lowest reliable business-class figure to Bangkok all year. The tradeoff is a brutal 22-24 hour door-to-door journey with Doha layovers of 3-7 hours.

## Q4: Can you fly nonstop to Bangkok from Europe or Australia?
Yes. Thai Airways flies nonstop from London Heathrow, Frankfurt, Munich, Zurich, and Copenhagen in around 11-12 hours, with LHR economy at £650-900 round-trip outside peak. From Sydney and Melbourne, Thai Airways and Qantas both fly nonstop in about nine hours, typically AUD $900-1,300 in shoulder season; Perth is seven hours.

## Q5: What are the best budget airlines to Bangkok within Asia?
AirAsia has the largest network into Don Mueang, with Kuala Lumpur-Bangkok one-ways often $40-70. Scoot flies Singapore-Bangkok from around $80, sometimes on more comfortable 787s. VietJet is cheap but often delayed. For flights over five hours, regional business on Singapore Airlines or Thai Airways at $250-350 one-way is worth the upgrade.
