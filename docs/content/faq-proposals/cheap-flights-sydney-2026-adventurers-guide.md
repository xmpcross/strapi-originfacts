## Q1: When are flights to Sydney cheapest in 2026?
Three windows deliver the lowest fares: February 10 through March 20, when US West Coast round-trips drop to $720–900; May 5 through June 15, when United's LAX–SYD has hit $812; and late October through November 20 before the Christmas surge. Avoid December 18 through January 10, when fares double, and Easter week.

## Q2: Which airline is cheapest for flying to Sydney from the US?
From the US West Coast, Fiji Airways via Nadi is the value pick at $680–950 round-trip on relatively new A350s, though layovers can stretch to six hours. Qantas nonstops run $1,100–1,500, United's cash fares start around $950 in shoulder season, and Delta's seasonal LAX nonstop shows $799 flash fares in February.

## Q3: Are there nonstop flights to Sydney from the US East Coast?
No — East Coast nonstops do not exist since JFK–SYD ended years ago. Options include American via DFW on Qantas aircraft at $1,250–1,600 with bags checked through, Korean Air via Incheon from JFK, often $1,100 round-trip, and Qatar Airways via Doha, which has shown $1,295 Qsuites business fares during October sales.

## Q4: Is it cheaper to fly into Melbourne instead of Sydney?
Melbourne often prices $150–250 below Sydney, and the gap can be closed with a one-hour Jetstar hop or the 11-hour XPT train. The tactic works especially well from Europe via Singapore. Split tickets, such as LAX–Nadi on Fiji Airways plus Nadi–Sydney on Virgin Australia, have beaten single tickets by over $200.

## Q5: How far in advance should you book Sydney flights for 2026?
Book by early November 2025 for February–March travel, January to mid-February 2026 for May–June, and April to June 2026 for October–November. When a fare sits within 10 percent of the historical low for that route and month, book immediately — last-minute Sydney deals are rare because planes fill and airlines do not panic-discount.
