# FAQ proposal — How to Find Cheap Flights to Bali Without Hidden Fees

## Q1: When is the cheapest time to fly to Bali?
Shoulder season — mid-January through March and again September–October — regularly produces the lowest fares, often under $700 round-trip from LAX or SFO with a connection. Peak season in July–August and the Christmas–New Year window pushes West Coast round-trips above $1,000, so those dates should be booked four to five months ahead.

## Q2: Are there nonstop flights from the US to Bali?
No — there are no nonstop flights from the US or Canada to Denpasar; every itinerary connects at least once. Common one-stop routings include Singapore Airlines via Changi at $700–1,000 round-trip from the West Coast, Cathay Pacific via Hong Kong, and China Southern or China Eastern, often the cheapest at under $600 from LAX.

## Q3: What hidden fees do budget airlines charge on Bali flights?
Baggage is the biggest trap: a 20kg checked bag on carriers like AirAsia, Scoot, or Lion Air can cost $30–60 each way, more at the airport. Seat selection adds $5–25 per seat per leg, payment surcharges remain common on Asian budget airline sites, and booking platforms often pre-tick travel insurance by default.

## Q4: Are points and miles worth using for Bali flights?
For long-haul US departures, yes: a Singapore Airlines business-class redemption from the West Coast runs roughly 100,000–130,000 KrisFlyer miles round-trip, while cash prices regularly exceed $3,000. Economy award space appears when schedules open 10–11 months out. From nearby countries like Australia or Malaysia, cash fares are too low for miles to make sense.

## Q5: How do you get from Bali's airport to your hotel?
Official metered taxi kiosks inside the international arrivals terminal charge fixed rates by zone — ask for a quote before heading to the parking area. Gojek and Grab cannot pick up inside the airport zone, but walking beyond the boundary road to request one typically saves money. Budget 30–60 minutes for immigration during peak arrival windows.
