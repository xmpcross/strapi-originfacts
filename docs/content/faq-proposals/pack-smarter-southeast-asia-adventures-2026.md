## Q1: What size bag should you bring to Southeast Asia?
A 40–50L carry-on-compatible travel backpack is the sweet spot for trips under three weeks; the Osprey Farpoint 40 and Nomatic Travel Pack are consistent favorites. Both fit overhead bins on Scoot, Vietjet, and AirAsia, avoiding checked-bag fees of $20–$40 per flight. Check current carry-on limits — 56cm x 36cm x 23cm and typically 7–10kg.

## Q2: What clothes should you pack for Southeast Asia adventures?
Follow the 3-2-1 system: three bottoms, two tops, one layer. Recommended items include two moisture-wicking shirts, a long-sleeve sun shirt that doubles as a temple cover, convertible pants, shorts, a lightweight packable layer for overnight buses, plus merino or synthetic underwear and wool-blend socks. Leave jeans, extra formal outfits, and full hiking boots at home.

## Q3: What shoes do you need for Southeast Asia?
Two pairs maximum: sandals with a heel strap, such as Chacos, Tevas, or Bedrock Cairns, which stay on during motorbike rides and wet boat decks; and trail runners like the Salomon Speedcross 6 or Hoka Speedgoat, which dry faster than boots after monsoon rain. Dedicated boots are only worth it for serious treks like Mulu's Pinnacles or Mount Rinjani.

## Q4: What health items are essential for Southeast Asia adventure travel?
Pack DEET repellent at 30 percent concentration — the CDC's effective threshold, with dengue active year-round — plus water purification tablets or a SteriPen for off-route trekking, and oral rehydration salts. Discuss altitude medication with a doctor for destinations like Mount Kinabalu. Carry travel insurance that explicitly covers trekking, diving, and motorbike riding, which standard policies often exclude.

## Q5: How should you handle money and cards in Southeast Asia?
Southeast Asia runs heavily on cash, and ATM access can be unreliable or expensive. A Charles Schwab debit card or Wise card avoids fees, while standard US debit cards can cost $5–$8 per withdrawal at Thai and Vietnamese ATMs. Split cash across three locations, and carry printed e-visa copies to prevent headaches at land borders.
