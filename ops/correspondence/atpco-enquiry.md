I run Originfacts (www.originfacts.com), a travel reference site. We publish

and cited on the page.

Before taking up your time, the question that probably decides this:

**Is there a subscription tier for a content publisher with no booking volume?**

We are not an airline, not an agency, and we do not sell tickets. If ATPCO's
commercial model is sized to transaction volume, that is a clean no and I would
rather hear it in one reply than after a discovery call.

If there is a fit, two products interest us:

1. **Baggage Allowance and Charges** — allowances, charges, size and weight
   limits per carrier. We currently read these from each carrier's own pages
   and verify them by hand, which does not scale past a few dozen airlines and
   goes stale silently.

2. **Routehappy Attributes** — seat type, pitch, width and legroom per subfleet
   and cabin. Nothing free and reliable exists for this.

Two things I would need to understand:

- **Display rights.** Can licensed values be published on a public reference
  page, and what attribution is required? Our pages cite a source for every
  figure, so we would need to know how to credit ATPCO correctly.

- **Change detection.** Even if display rights are constrained, a feed that
  tells us *when a carrier changes an allowance* would be valuable on its own —
  we would keep citing the carrier's page and use the feed to know when to
  re-verify. If that is a cheaper or simpler arrangement, it may suit us better
  than full display licensing.
