## Q1: When do Bangkok flights get cheap in 2026?
The lowest fares appear late April through mid-June, during Thailand's hot season, when Los Angeles round-trips have dropped to $580 — about $300 below peak. A secondary window runs September through mid-October, with New York fares typically $650–750. Avoid December through February, mid-October through November, and Songkran week in mid-April.

## Q2: Which US cities have the cheapest flights to Bangkok?
West Coast airports consistently beat East Coast fares by $100–200. Los Angeles is cheapest overall with multiple daily options, San Francisco runs a close second, and Seattle is an underrated hub with competitive Asian carrier pricing. Chicago is the best Midwest option, while New York offers the most frequency at higher base fares.

## Q3: How far in advance should you book flights to Bangkok?
Bangkok routes do not follow the typical six-to-eight-week booking window. Asian carriers release their lowest inventory three to four months ahead of departure, and prices climb steadily afterward, so starting the search 11–14 weeks out wins consistently. Tuesday departures and Wednesday returns run $50–100 lower than weekend pricing when schedules allow.

## Q4: Which airlines offer the best value for Bangkok flights?
EVA Air via Taipei offers consistently good service at competitive prices, while China Southern's Guangzhou connections deliver rock-bottom fares with longer journeys. Korean Air provides premium service through Seoul at reasonable premiums, Thai Airways flies direct from LAX for roughly $100–150 more, and ANA's Tokyo connections work well for earning Star Alliance miles.

## Q5: How do you get from Bangkok's airport to downtown, and what does arrival cost?
The Airport Rail Link from Suvarnabhumi to downtown runs every 15 minutes and costs under $2, connecting directly to BTS Skytrain lines — the $25 taxi is only worth it after midnight. Visa on arrival for US citizens costs about $35 and takes 20–30 minutes during normal hours; immigration lines otherwise move quickly.
