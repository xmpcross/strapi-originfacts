## Q1: How much do cheap flights to the UK cost in 2026?
Round-trip economy fares from major US cities have regularly appeared between roughly $380 and $560 in 2026. Examples include JFK–Heathrow at $380–$520, LAX–Gatwick at $420–$560, Chicago–Manchester at $380–$470, Boston under $450 in autumn, Miami at $390–$490, Philadelphia at $400–$500, and Seattle via Reykjavik at $380–$480.

## Q2: When is the cheapest time to fly to the UK?
January, February, and early March are the cheapest months, with October and early November close behind. The lowest fares typically appear in the six-to-ten-week window before departure, and Tuesday and Wednesday departures run $60–$100 cheaper than Friday and Sunday flights on the same route.

## Q3: Do Americans need any authorization to visit the UK in 2026?
Yes. The UK's Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) scheme is live in 2026 for US passport holders. It costs £10 and must be approved before flying, so applications should go through the official UK government website at least a week ahead of the departure date.

## Q4: Is it cheaper to fly into Gatwick or Manchester instead of Heathrow?
Often, yes. LAX–Gatwick consistently undercuts LAX–Heathrow by $60–$120 round-trip because Gatwick handles lower-cost carriers. Chicago–Manchester has touched $380–$470 in shoulder season, and Manchester's rail links reach Liverpool in 35 minutes, York in about an hour, and Edinburgh in roughly two and a half hours.

## Q5: Is Norse Atlantic a good budget option for UK flights?
Norse Atlantic changed the transatlantic market with no-frills basic economy — carry-on bags and seat selection cost extra. For budget-first travelers, paying $400 round-trip plus $40 for a bag still beats $700 on a legacy carrier. Norse operates on the JFK–Heathrow route and flies LAX–Gatwick directly.
