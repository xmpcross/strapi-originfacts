# FAQ proposal — How to Find Cheap Car Rentals Without Getting Burned by Hidden Fees

Slug: `cheap-car-rentals-avoid-hidden-fees` · Status: **PROPOSED — not in Strapi** · Grounding: article body only

## Q1: What are the best tools for finding cheap car rental rates?
Costco Travel consistently undercuts the major booking engines by 10–20 percent, and its rates usually include liability coverage and sometimes a second driver. AutoSlash monitors an existing reservation and rebooks automatically when a cheaper rate appears, for free. Rentalcars.com and Priceline's opaque Express Deals are worth running as parallel comparisons.

## Q2: What hidden fees do car rental companies add at the counter?
Typical counter additions include a collision damage waiver at $15–30 per day, supplemental liability insurance at $10–15 per day, fuel pre-pay schemes, young driver surcharges of $25–35 daily for drivers under 25, additional driver fees around $10–15 per day, and airport facility fees adding 10–30 percent in concession charges and taxes.

## Q3: Does credit card insurance replace the rental company's damage waiver?
Often, yes. Cards like the Chase Sapphire Reserve and Preferred offer primary rental car insurance, letting renters decline the counter CDW/LDW, while the Amex Platinum provides secondary coverage. The rental must be paid entirely with the card, and exclusions apply — most cards will not cover trucks, exotic cars, or rentals over 30 days.

## Q4: Is it cheaper to rent a car away from the airport?
Airport rentals carry concession and facility charges that can add 20–30 percent to the base rate. A neighborhood branch a few miles away often prices the same car class noticeably lower in cities like Los Angeles, Chicago, or Miami. Factor in rideshare costs to reach it; trips of five or more days usually still come out ahead.

## Q5: When should you book a rental car for the best price?
Book early, then re-check since most rental reservations cancel and rebook for free — once two to three weeks out and again three to four days before pickup. Holidays like July 4th, Thanksgiving, and Memorial Day weekend spike prices hard, so book those as early as three months out when dates are known.
