# FAQ proposal — Best Australia Road Trip Car Rentals to Book Early in 2026

## Q1: When should you book an Australian rental car for 2026?
For peak windows — the April 3-6 Easter weekend, the June 27-July 12 school holidays and dry-season peak, spring holidays September 19-October 4, and the December-January coastal rush — book by late January or early February 2026. Outside those windows, three to four weeks ahead is usually fine; 4WDs and campervans need four to six months.

## Q2: Which rental companies are best for Australian road trips?
East Coast Car Rentals and Bayswater undercut the majors on sedans out of Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and Gold Coast airports. Hertz, Avis, Budget, and Europcar win for one-way and remote pickups thanks to deeper networks. For outback 4WD trips, Britz, Apollo, and Outback Car Hire supply vehicles permitted on unsealed roads.

## Q3: Do you need a 4WD for an Australian road trip?
Only for unsealed routes like the Gibb River Road, Mereenie Loop, Cape York, the Oodnadatta Track, or the Kimberley off the bitumen — standard rentals typically void insurance the moment you leave a sealed surface. The Great Ocean Road, Sydney-to-Cairns, and the main Tasmania loop are all fine in a 2WD sedan or small SUV.

## Q4: How can you save on rental car excess insurance in Australia?
Standard insurance leaves a $3,000-5,500 excess on a 2WD and $5,000-7,500 on a 4WD, and operators charge $25-45 per day to remove it. Instead, buy a standalone excess policy from Tripcover or RentalCover.com at roughly $9-15 per day, decline the counter offer, and take date-stamped photos of the vehicle at pickup and dropoff.

## Q5: How much do campervan rentals cost in Australia?
Backpacker-style vans from Jucy and Spaceships run about $80-140 per day in shoulder season and $150-220 at peak, with Travellers Autobarn slightly cheaper. Premium Maui and Britz motorhomes with showers and toilets start near $250 per day and pass $400 in peak. Note that many free campsites require a self-contained vehicle with an onboard toilet.
