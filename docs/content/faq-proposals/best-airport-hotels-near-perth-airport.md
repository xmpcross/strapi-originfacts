## Q1: Which hotel is closest to Perth Airport?
The ibis Perth Airport is the only hotel physically on the airport precinct, attached to the international/domestic terminal complex via a covered walkway and about a 10-minute walk to Terminal 1 check-in — no shuttle required. Standard rooms typically run under $200 per night, though prices spike during school holidays and major events.

## Q2: Do Perth Airport hotels have free shuttles?
The Crowne Plaza Perth Airport in Redcliffe runs a complimentary 24-hour shuttle, considered one of the city's more reliable airport shuttle operations, with the hotel a 5-8 minute drive from both terminal precincts. Confirm the drop-off point for Terminal 3 or 4 flights, since not every run serves both precincts. Belmont apartments generally offer no shuttle.

## Q3: Is a park-and-fly package at Perth Airport worth it?
It depends on trip length. For trips of five days or longer, a package bundling one night's accommodation with parking can beat Perth Airport's premium long-term rates; on a 2-night trip it rarely makes sense. Crowne Plaza frequently offers duration parking packages, and the ibis has on-site parking. Always compare total costs before assuming a deal.

## Q4: Which terminal does my airline use at Perth Airport?
Terminals 1 and 2 handle international and Qantas domestic flights, while Terminals 3 and 4 — a separate building about 3 kilometres away — serve Virgin Australia and other domestic carriers. Because the precincts are not adjacent, always confirm your departure terminal before choosing a hotel based on walking distance or shuttle convenience.

## Q5: What are the budget options near Perth Airport?
Belmont and Cloverdale, a short rideshare from the terminals, have budget accommodation including independent motels under $120 per night, with rideshares to the airport usually under $25. Booking the ibis off-peak also cuts rates. For layovers under eight hours, Qantas Club, Virgin Lounge, or Plaza Premium members can use lounge facilities — Terminal 1's Plaza Premium has showers.
