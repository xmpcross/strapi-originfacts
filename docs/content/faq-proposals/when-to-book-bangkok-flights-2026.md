# FAQ proposal — When to Book Bangkok Flights in 2026 for Big Savings

## Q1: How far in advance should you book flights to Bangkok in 2026?
Book 10–14 weeks out from North America and 8–12 weeks from Europe, when carriers such as Cathay Pacific, EVA, ANA, Korean Air, Qatar, and Turkish load meaningful sales. From Australia, 6–10 weeks suffices; within Asia, 3–6 weeks. For peaks like Christmas or Songkran, book four to six months ahead.

## Q2: What are the cheapest months to fly to Bangkok in 2026?
Mid-January through February, mid-May through early June, and September through early October carry the lowest fares. September–October offers the deepest long-haul economy discounts despite heavy rain. Avoid mid-December to January 5, Songkran (fares stay elevated April 8–20), and late-April Golden Week for itineraries connecting through Tokyo or Osaka.

## Q3: Which airlines have the best deals to Bangkok?
EVA Air is quietly the best long-haul value from the US West Coast, with twice-yearly sales dropping LAX/SFO round-trips to roughly $750–$850; China Airlines prices similarly. Qatar Airways serves Europe at £450–£600 round-trip in shoulder months, while ZIPAIR flies Tokyo Narita to Bangkok often under $250 one-way.

## Q4: Is flying into Don Mueang cheaper than Suvarnabhumi?
Yes for regional routes — Don Mueang (DMK) is Bangkok's low-cost hub, served heavily by AirAsia, Nok Air, Thai Lion, and Scoot, with fares 15–30% cheaper than the same routes into Suvarnabhumi. Metered taxis to Sukhumvit run about 350–500 baht, or the A1 bus reaches Mo Chit BTS for 30 baht.

## Q5: Does the day of the week affect Bangkok flight prices?
The Tuesday-is-cheapest myth is mostly dead, since revenue management now follows demand curves, but Tuesday, Wednesday, and Saturday departures from North America still tend to run cheaper, with Friday and Sunday the worst. Red-eyes and awkward connections with four-hour-plus layovers often shave another $80–$150 off the fare.
