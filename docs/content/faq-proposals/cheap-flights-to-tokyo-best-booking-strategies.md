## Q1: When is the best time to book flights to Tokyo?
Book two to four months before departure for standard travel periods, extending to four to six months for peak seasons like cherry blossoms, Golden Week, and New Year. The best value windows are late January through February and late September through mid-October, when Pacific fares are most competitive.

## Q2: How much do cheap flights to Tokyo cost?
Under $700 round-trip from the US West Coast is a repeatable outcome, while sub-$900 is the realistic sweet spot from the East Coast. ANA fare sales have hit under $600 round-trip from LAX, and fares under $650 from the West Coast are worth booking without hesitation.

## Q3: Is Haneda or Narita better for flying into Tokyo?
Haneda is closer to central Tokyo — about 30 minutes by monorail to Hamamatsucho versus Narita's 60–90-minute Narita Express ride to Shinjuku — and usually saves $10–$15 on the ground transfer. However, Narita fares occasionally run cheaper, especially on budget carriers, so pricing both airports is recommended.

## Q4: Which airlines have the cheapest flights to Tokyo?
ANA consistently offers some of the best economy fares from the US, particularly from Los Angeles, San Francisco, and New York, with Japan Airlines pricing comparably. Korean Air and Asiana, routing through Seoul, frequently undercut direct services by $100–$200, while Scoot offers budget legs from Southeast Asian hubs.

## Q5: How many miles does an award flight to Tokyo cost?
A round-trip economy award from the continental US through ANA Mileage Club typically runs around 55,000 miles, transferable from Amex Membership Rewards. Business-class availability on ANA and JAL books up 11 months out and is competitive, so flexible dates matter; for fixed cherry blossom weeks, cash may be more practical.
