## Q1: How much do TSA PreCheck and Global Entry cost?
TSA PreCheck costs $78 for five years and provides dedicated lanes at more than 200 airports, with waits typically under ten minutes and no need to remove shoes, belts, or laptops. Global Entry costs $100 for five years and includes PreCheck benefits plus expedited customs on international returns. Applications take two to four weeks.

## Q2: Is CLEAR worth it compared to TSA PreCheck?
CLEAR verifies identity with fingerprint and iris scans, letting members skip the ID-check line and go straight to physical screening. At $189 annually it costs more than PreCheck, though credit cards often discount it, and the two pair well for maximum efficiency. CLEAR operates at over 50 U.S. airports and is expanding in 2026.

## Q3: What time of day are airport security lines shortest?
Lines are typically shortest between 5:00 and 6:30 AM and after 8:00 PM. Business travelers create rushes from 7:00–9:00 AM and 4:00–7:00 PM, so shifting a departure by even an hour can avoid peak congestion. Apps like MyTSA also show real-time wait estimates for planning ahead.

## Q4: How does packing help you get through security faster?
Following the 3-1-1 rule — liquids in 3.4-ounce containers, one quart-sized bag, one bag per passenger — prevents common screening delays. Organizing electronics in easy-access pockets, wearing slip-on shoes, and minimizing jewelry also speeds things up. Roughly thirty seconds spent packing smart can save around ten minutes at the checkpoint.

## Q5: Can you use a digital ID at airport security?
Several states offer digital driver's licenses accepted at TSA checkpoints — currently Arizona, Colorado, and Maryland, with acceptance expanding through 2026. Combined with a mobile boarding pass instead of a paper one, travelers can pass through with only a phone, avoiding any fumbling for documents at the checkpoint.
