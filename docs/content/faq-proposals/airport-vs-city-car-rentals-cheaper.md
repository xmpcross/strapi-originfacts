# FAQ proposal — Airport vs City Car Rentals: Which Is Actually Cheaper?

Slug: `airport-vs-city-car-rentals-cheaper` · Status: **PROPOSED — not in Strapi** · Grounding: article body only

## Q1: Are airport car rentals more expensive than city branches?
Airport rentals can cost 20-30% more than the same car booked at a city branch a few miles away. Mandatory surcharges — a concession recovery fee of roughly 10-11% of the base rate, a customer facility charge of $6-$10 per day, access fees, and higher local taxes — can add $60-$90 to a $200 base rate.

## Q2: When is renting at the airport worth it?
The airport counter makes sense for short rentals under 48 hours, late-night arrivals when city branches have closed, one-way rentals where off-airport drop fees can exceed $200, travelers with elite status programs that only operate at airport locations, and business trips where the rental is expensed and time matters more than price.

## Q3: How much can you save with a city branch rental?
On longer rentals the savings are substantial. In Orlando, a 7-day mid-size SUV cost about $520 at the airport versus roughly $380 at a Kissimmee Enterprise branch — around $105 saved even after a $35 Uber. On a 2-day Las Vegas weekend, the difference shrank to $7, making the airport counter the better choice.

## Q4: How do you compare airport and city rental prices quickly?
Search the airport code on AutoSlash, Kayak, or Costco Travel and note the all-in total, then search a city branch of the same operator 5-15 miles away with the same dates and car class. If the difference is under $40, stay at the airport; at $50 or more, check rideshare costs, branch hours, and return shuttle logistics.

## Q5: What can go wrong with the city-branch strategy?
Common pitfalls include branches that don't shuttle from the airport, leaving you with a $40 one-way rideshare; thin inventory at smaller city fleets; sneaky access fees at branches inside the airport perimeter; and a 30-45 minute return-shuttle delay before departure. Always confirm shuttle service by phone and check closing hours, especially on Sundays.
